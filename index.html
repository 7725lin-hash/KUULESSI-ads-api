Privacy Notice

Company: KUULESSI TECHNOLOGY LIMITED

Effective date: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Notice explains how KUULESSI TECHNOLOGY LIMITED handles information in connection with its internal business applications, including applications that may connect to Amazon services and APIs for the management, analysis, and reporting of the Company's own business and advertising activities.

1. Information We Process

Our internal applications may process business and operational information made available through authorized services and APIs. Depending on the service, this may include:

  • Account and profile identifiers;
  • Product, listing, inventory, pricing, and operational information;
  • Advertising campaigns, ad groups, ads, keywords, targeting, budgets, and bids;
  • Search term, placement, and performance report data;
  • Metrics such as impressions, clicks, spend, orders, sales, CPC, ACOS, and ROAS;
  • Report metadata and other information required for internal analysis and management.

Our internal applications are not intended to collect or use buyer payment card information or other sensitive payment credentials.

2. How We Use Information

We use information only for legitimate internal business purposes, including:

  • Business reporting and performance analysis;
  • Advertising campaign monitoring and optimization;
  • Inventory, product, pricing, and operational management;
  • Financial reconciliation and planning;
  • Internal dashboards, alerts, and decision support.

3. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell information obtained through authorized APIs or services. Information is not made available to unaffiliated advertisers, sellers, agencies, or other external users except where required by law, necessary to protect legal rights, or required for the secure operation of services used by the Company.

4. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect business information, credentials, and access tokens. Access is limited to authorized personnel based on business need. Credentials and tokens are not intended to be stored in public repositories, exposed in client-side code, or shared publicly.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for legitimate business, reporting, security, and legal purposes. Information that is no longer required will be deleted, anonymized, or securely archived in accordance with our internal procedures and applicable requirements.

6. Authorization and Access Revocation

Where an application uses an authorization mechanism such as Login with Amazon or another OAuth-based service, access may be revoked through the applicable account authorization settings. Revoking access prevents the application from obtaining new information through the revoked authorization.

7. Changes to This Notice

We may update this Privacy Notice when our applications, data practices, or legal obligations change. The effective date shown above will be updated when a revised version is published.
